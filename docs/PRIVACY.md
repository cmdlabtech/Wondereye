# Wondereye Privacy Notice

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

This notice describes the information Wondereye uses and how it is shown on the public map at wondereye.app/map.

## Location
Wondereye uses your location to find places near you. Coordinates are rounded to approximately 110 metres before they are used to look up nearby places. Your location is not stored with any identifier and is not shown on the map.

## Anonymous explorer number
When the app first runs, it creates a random number on your device, for example "Explorer #1234". When you are the first person to discover a place, this number and the date are attached to that place and shown publicly on the map as "First discovered by Explorer #1234". Because the same number is used for every place you first discover, those places can be seen as discovered by the same explorer. The number is not derived from, and is not linked to, your name, your Even Realities account, or your device.

## What we do not collect
Wondereye does not collect your name, your Even Realities account information, your email address, or device identifiers such as serial numbers.

## Places on the map
Place names, descriptions, and coordinates come from public sources, including OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia, and are shown publicly on the map.

## Contact
For questions about this notice, or to request removal of a label from a place, please use the feedback form at wondereye.app.
